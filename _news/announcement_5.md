title: Research visit to DKFZ Heidelberg (January–May 2026)
---

From January to May 2026, I was a visiting PhD researcher at the [German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ)](https://www.dkfz.de/en/index.html) in Heidelberg, Germany, working with the [Medical Image Computing (MIC)](https://www.dkfz.de/en/mic/index.php) team led by [Klaus Maier-Hein](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oCrBpVMAAAAJ) on uncertainty estimation and reliable medical image segmentation.

This visit was funded by the **CNRS GdR IASIS Doctoral Mobility Grant**.
