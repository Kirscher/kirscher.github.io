Invited talk given at the **Séminaires ICube IMAGeS** ([IMAGeS team](https://images.icube.unistra.fr/), ICube Laboratory, University of Strasbourg) on June 3rd, 2026.

**Title:** Toward Reliable Uncertainty in Medical Image Segmentation

The presentation covers recent methodological contributions on calibration and uncertainty quantification in medical image analysis, including multi-rater calibration, uncertainty estimation via model ensembles, and post-hoc calibration in dense segmentation, and their relationship with segmentation metrics.
