Our paper **Rethinking Post-Hoc Calibration in Semantic Segmentation** has been accepted at [Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR)](https://openreview.net/forum?id=xwNoSNxgxV). It studies two principles for post-hoc calibrators: **translation invariance** and **decision preservation**.

Alongside the paper we release **Fiducio**, an open-source Python library implementing the calibrators studied there, for classification as well as 2D and 3D semantic segmentation.

- 📄 Paper & project page: [fiducio-ai.github.io/Fiducio/paper](https://fiducio-ai.github.io/Fiducio/paper)
- 💻 Code: [github.com/fiducio-ai/Fiducio](https://github.com/fiducio-ai/Fiducio)
- 📦 Install: `pip install fiducio`
- 🎉 Announcement on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tristan-k-_machinelearning-computervision-medicalimaging-activity-7508803350092480529-HFvC)

Joint work with Kim-Celine Kahl, Bálint Kovács, Maximilian Rokuss, Klaus Maier-Hein, Xavier Coubez, Philippe Meyer and Sylvain Faisan — ICube Strasbourg, Université de Strasbourg, Institut Strauss and MIC @ DKFZ.
